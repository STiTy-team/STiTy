[Order Principles]
- When one candidate keeps the main verb and its object together while another splits verb from object, prefer the cut that preserves verb+object (keep predicate integrity).
- When one cut yields a full independent clause after the marker and another cuts earlier inside a long noun phrase, prefer the later cut that delivers a full clause to the listener.
- When one position separates a subordinating opener from its head clause and another keeps the dependent clause intact, prefer the cut that keeps the dependent clause attached to its head.
